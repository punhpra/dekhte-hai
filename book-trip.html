Frequently asked questions

Does dekhtehai charge for flight search or booking?

No. Searching and booking here is free. If you book through one of our partner links, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you — that's what keeps the price alerts free.

Why might prices differ from other sites?

Results come from our booking partners' live inventory, which can differ slightly from other search engines due to timing, currency, or fare-class availability. Always confirm the final price on the booking page before paying.

Is it safe to book through these links?

Yes — the search and booking widgets on this page are provided by established travel booking partners; we don't process payments or handle your booking details ourselves.

I'd rather wait for a lower price — can I do that instead?

Yes, set a free price alert on our Deals page and we'll email you when the fare drops, rather than booking right away.
