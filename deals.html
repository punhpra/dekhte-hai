Frequently asked questions

How often do you check prices?

We check most routes every couple of hours. You're only emailed when a genuinely lower price is confirmed — not on a fixed schedule, so an empty inbox just means nothing's changed.

Is dekhtehai free to use?

Yes, setting up and receiving alerts is free. Some booking links in our emails are affiliate links — if you book through one we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you, which is what keeps the alerts running.

How many routes can I track?

Each email address can have up to 2 active routes at a time on the free tier.

How do I stop or change an alert?

Every alert email includes a link to manage or delete that specific alert — no account or login needed.
