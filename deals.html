What you'll get

  • We check your route once a day and compare it to the lowest fare we've seen for you so far.
  • You'll only get an email when a genuinely lower price shows up — not a daily digest, so an empty inbox just means nothing's changed.
  • Right now we search round-trip, direct flights, for stays of roughly 1–4 weeks. One-way trips and flights with stopovers aren't covered yet.
  • Hotel price alerts are on the way — for now this covers flights only.
  • Currently it is limited to 2 routes per email.
