Tell us your route and, if you like, the price you're waiting for. We'll email you the moment we find a fare that matches.

Start typing a city or airport name, then pick one from the list. Cities with multiple airports (e.g. London, New York, Tokyo) offer an "All airports" option that searches all of them for the cheapest fare.

Leave the price blank and we'll email you about any drop we find, shown in your chosen currency.

Glad this could help! If a price alert saves you money on your next trip, you can support the project with a coffee — it helps keep the alerts running.

We check your route once a day and only email you when a genuinely lower price shows up — not a daily digest, so an empty inbox just means nothing's changed. Right now this covers round-trip, direct flights for stays of roughly 1–4 weeks; one-way trips and flights with stopovers aren't covered yet, and hotel price alerts are on the way. Each email address is currently limited to 2 active routes.
